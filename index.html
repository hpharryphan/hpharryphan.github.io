6-DOF Rover Manipulator Arm

Arm for URC 2027, built by a joint US-Japan team around swappable joint modules that can be serviced in the field.

PeriodSep 2026 – Present
RoleMechanical Design Engineer
TeamKARURA URC Team
ToolsFusion 360, DFM/DFA, GD&T drawings
Objectives, details & gallery

Objectives

  • Carry a 5 kg payload (8 kg stretch limit)
  • Reach with a link span of about 1.17 m
  • Move joints at 45°/s
  • Make faults quick to isolate and fix in the field

Design details

  • Six degrees of freedom with fully swappable joint modules
  • Cycloidal reducers and timing belts, up to 420:1 reduction
  • Independently actuated wrist joints for rapid fault isolation
  • DFM and DFA applied for CNC, waterjet, and SLS/FDM 3D-printed parts
  • 2D drawings with GD&T on mounting interfaces and bearing bores

Status

  • Ongoing: designed for URC 2027 with the KARURA URC Team.
